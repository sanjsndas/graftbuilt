    Before you write

    Do you accept guest articles or story pitches?
    Yes, we welcome pitches from architects, designers, and writers. Please send a short synopsis (150–200 words) and a link to your portfolio or previous work to pitch@graftbuilideas.com. We respond to every serious pitch.
    How can I advertise or partner with Graft Built Ideas?
    We offer limited, design-led partnership opportunities. For media kits and rates, write to hello@graftbuilideas.com with the subject line “Partnership”. We’re particularly interested in collaborations with sustainable brands and independent makers.
    Can I republish or translate an article?
    We occasionally allow translations or excerpts. Please reach out with the specific article and your platform details. All content is copyrighted, so we ask that you don’t republish without written permission.
    How do I report a factual error or typo?
    Accuracy matters to us. Send an email to hello@graftbuilideas.com with the subject “Correction” and include the article link and the specific detail. We’ll review and update promptly.
