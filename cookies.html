    Cookies Policy

    We use cookies to make our site work better for you. This page explains what they are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

    This Cookies Policy describes how Graft Built Ideas ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on graftbuilideas.com (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal information more broadly.

    By continuing to browse the Site, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy. If you do not agree, you can disable cookies in your browser settings (see section 6) or discontinue use of the Site.

    In short: We use a small number of cookies. Essential ones keep the site running, analytics cookies help us understand what readers enjoy, and preference cookies remember your choices. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to provide information to site owners, and to remember your preferences.

    Cookies can be "session cookies" (which are deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them). They can also be "first-party cookies" (set by the site you are visiting) or "third-party cookies" (set by a domain other than the one you are visiting).

    2. How we use cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Essential functionality: To ensure the Site loads correctly, to remember your cookie consent choice, and to maintain basic security.
    • Analytics and performance: To understand how visitors interact with the Site — which pages are most popular, how long people stay, and how they navigate. This helps us improve our content and user experience.
    • Preferences: To remember settings you have chosen, such as language or display preferences, so you don't have to re-enter them on every visit.

    We do not use cookies for advertising, retargeting, or selling your data to third parties.

    3. Types of cookies we use

    The table below lists the specific cookies we may place on your device, along with their purpose and duration.

    Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
    cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preference so we don't ask on every visit. 12 months
    session_id Essential Maintains a secure session while you browse the Site. Session
    _ga / _ga_* Analytics Used by Google Analytics to distinguish unique users and track page views (anonymized IP). Up to 26 months
    plausible (if used) Analytics Privacy-friendly analytics that does not collect personal data or use cross-site tracking. Session
    pref_theme Preference Remembers display preferences (e.g., light/dark mode) if you adjust them. 6 months

    Note: We may update the specific cookies we use from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this table, and the "Last updated" date will be revised.

    4. Third-party cookies

    Some cookies may be set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We currently use:

    • Google Analytics (or a similar privacy-focused alternative) to measure site traffic and usage patterns. Google may also transfer this information to third parties where required to do so by law. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
    • Plausible Analytics (if used) — a lightweight, privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies at all in some configurations, or uses anonymized data only.

    We do not control third-party cookies directly. You should check the relevant third-party websites for more information about their cookies and how to manage them.

    5. Your cookie choices

    When you first visit the Site, you will see a cookie banner or notice asking for your consent to non-essential cookies. You can:

    • Accept all cookies: This allows us to use analytics and preference cookies to improve your experience.
    • Reject non-essential cookies: Only essential cookies will be used. This may limit some functionality.
    • Customize your preferences: You can choose which categories of cookies to allow (where available).

    You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer, or by clearing your browser cookies and refreshing the page.

    6. How to manage cookies in your browser

    Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

    • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually or in bulk.
    • Block third-party cookies.
    • Block all cookies from specific sites.
    • Block all cookies entirely.
    • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

    To manage cookies in your browser, look for the "Settings", "Preferences", or "Privacy" section. The following links may help:

    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari
    • Microsoft Edge

    Please note that if you disable cookies, some parts of the Site may not function properly, and your experience may be degraded.

    7. Do Not Track signals

    Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. We do not currently respond to DNT signals because there is no common industry standard for how to interpret them. However, we do not engage in cross-site tracking or advertising, so your privacy is respected regardless.

    8. Changes to this Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will provide a more prominent notice (such as a banner on the Site). We encourage you to review this page periodically.
