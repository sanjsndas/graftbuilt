    Frequently Asked Questions

    What's the first step in planning a home renovation?
    Start with a thorough assessment of your existing space: measure everything, note natural light, and identify what works and what doesn't. Then define a clear brief — how you want to live in the space — before thinking about aesthetics. This prevents costly changes later.
    How do I choose the right lighting for a living room?
    Layer your lighting. Use ambient light (ceiling or recessed) for general illumination, task lighting (floor lamps, table lamps) for reading, and accent lighting (wall sconces, picture lights) to highlight art or architecture. Dimmers are essential for flexibility.
    What are the most sustainable materials for interiors?
    Look for materials that are renewable, low-carbon, and locally sourced: reclaimed wood, bamboo, cork, natural linoleum, clay plaster, and recycled metal or glass. Also consider durability — the longer a material lasts, the more sustainable it is.
    How can I make a small room feel larger?
    Use light colors, avoid heavy curtains, choose furniture with legs (to show floor), use mirrors strategically, and keep sightlines clear. Vertical storage and multi-functional furniture also help. Most importantly, don't overfill the space.
